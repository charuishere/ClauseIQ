5. MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, plumbing, and HVAC
systems. Tenant shall be responsible for routine upkeep, replacing light
bulbs and air filters, and promptly reporting any needed repairs.

6. EARLY TERMINATION
Tenant may terminate this Lease early by providing sixty (60) days written
notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent at
the then-current rate.

7. PET POLICY
Pets are permitted with prior written Landlord approval and payment of the
monthly pet fee listed in Section 4. Maximum of two (2) pets per unit.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

first written above.
