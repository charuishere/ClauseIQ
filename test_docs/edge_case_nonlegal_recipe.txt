Classic Weeknight Chicken Tikka Masala

Serves 4 | Prep time: 20 minutes | Cook time: 35 minutes

Ingredients:
- 700g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 tsp ground cumin, divided
- 3 tsp paprika, divided
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine yogurt, lemon juice, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika,
   turmeric, and a pinch of salt. Add chicken pieces and marinate for at
   least 1 hour, ideally overnight in the refrigerator.

2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Remove chicken
   from marinade and cook until browned on all sides, about 6-8 minutes.
   Set aside.

3. In the same skillet, heat remaining oil and saute onion until soft and
   golden, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 more minute.

4. Stir in remaining cumin, paprika, and garam masala. Cook for 30 seconds
   until fragrant.

5. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.

6. Stir in heavy cream and return chicken to the skillet. Simmer for 10-15
   minutes until chicken is cooked through and sauce has thickened.

7. Season with salt to taste. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot
   with basmati rice or naan bread.

Tips:
- For a spicier version, add 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper with the other spices.
- This dish tastes even better the next day, making it great for meal prep.
- Substitute coconut milk for heavy cream for a dairy-free version.
